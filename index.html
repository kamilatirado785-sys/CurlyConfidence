WHY I CREATED CURLYCONFIDENCE

A place for every child to feel confident caring for their hair.

I’m a high school student interested in healthcare, patient education, and improving children’s experiences in medical settings. CurlyConfidence grew from my own curly-hair learning journey and a hope that children and caregivers can keep familiar routines during a hospital stay.

The goal is simple: make curly and textured hair education easier to access when a child is away from home and their normal routine.
